Where we're heading

Our goal: AI agent phone control that stays private and open

Make every phone a first-class citizen of the AI agent ecosystem — privately, openly, and beautifully.

The mission

AI agents are becoming the way people get digital work done. But the agent revolution has a blind spot: mobile. Phones are where life actually happens — apps, messages, payments, photos — yet agents can barely see them.

Our goal is to close that gap completely: any agent, any phone, live sight and full control, without a privacy trade-off. No cloud middleman. No account with us. No data we could leak, because we never hold any. Screenshots your agent asks for do go to that agent's model provider — see our Privacy Policy and Data Policy for the full picture.

When ScreenSync MCP succeeds, "can your AI check something on my phone?" becomes as normal as sharing your screen on a video call — and as private as doing it on your own Wi-Fi.
