Android MCP servers compared

As of 1 October 2026

An Android MCP server lets an AI agent such as Claude see and control an Android phone through the Model Context Protocol. The eight servers below differ in how they capture the screen, whether they need ADB or a relay, whether they also cover iOS, and what they cost. Each fact links to the project's own page.

Disclosure: ScreenSync MCP is our product. We have tried to describe every other project fairly and only from its own documentation. Spotted an error? Email info@epsoldev.com and we will fix it.

Quick picks

  • Live view of your own phone plus your real browser tab, kept on your network: ScreenSync MCP.
  • No ADB, and control from anywhere through a relay: ScreenMCP.
  • iOS and Android, including simulators and emulators: mobile-mcp.
  • Fast screenshots and input if you already use scrcpy: scrcpy-mcp.
  • Deep debugging: logs, files, apps and bug reports: DroidMind.
  • Phones in the cloud, no device of your own: mobilerun.

When to choose which

Choose ScreenSync MCP if…

you want your agent to watch your own Android phone live and also work in your real Chrome or Edge tab, from one free hub on your computer. Capture needs no root and no ADB; the tap, swipe and type tools use ADB over USB or wireless debugging. Frames stay on your network (screenshots your agent requests are sent to that agent's model provider), there is no per-command quota, and the tool count splits into 29 phone/desktop tools + 197 browser tools. It does not support iOS, and it expects the phone and computer on the same network unless you turn on the Google Drive fallback. Start with the ScreenSync MCP setup guide.

Choose ScreenMCP if…

you cannot or do not want to enable ADB, or you need to reach the phone when it is not on your network. Its accessibility-service app talks to a relay, so it works across networks, and you can self-host the relay if you prefer. It also controls Windows, macOS and Linux machines. The hosted plans meter commands per day.

Choose mobile-mcp if…

you need iOS as well as Android, or you test on simulators and emulators. Its accessibility-first approach reads the UI tree before falling back to screenshots, which keeps image tokens down.

Choose scrcpy-mcp if…

you already rely on scrcpy and want the fastest ADB-based screenshots and input, with a large set of 44 tools covering apps, files, the shell and media streaming.

Choose DroidMind if…

your work is device debugging: logs (logcat, ANR, crash, battery), files, app installs and bug reports. Its command validation and risk assessment add a safety layer around ADB.

Choose Android-MCP or android-mcp-server if…

you want a small Python codebase you can read and extend. Android-MCP works over USB or Wi-Fi ADB on Android 10+ devices and emulators; android-mcp-server keeps to five tools, one of which runs any ADB command.

Choose mobilerun if…

you do not want a physical device at all. Its hosted MCP server hands your agent real Android and iOS phones in the cloud, which suits CI and server-side agents. The open-source framework also drives local devices.

How we compared

On 1 October 2026 we read each project's README, website and pricing page, linked under its name in the table. We recorded only what those pages state; where a page is silent we wrote “not stated” rather than guessing. We did not run benchmarks, and we do not publish speed claims for other projects beyond the numbers they publish themselves. ScreenSync MCP's own numbers come from our product: for example, frames arrive in about 94 ms median on our LAN tests.

Projects change quickly. If a row is out of date, write to info@epsoldev.com with a link and we will update the page.
