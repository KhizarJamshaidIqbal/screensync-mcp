The people behind the product

About EpsolDev, the team behind ScreenSync MCP

We build tools that make AI genuinely useful — starting with giving it eyes and hands on the device you use most.

Our story

ScreenSync MCP started with a simple frustration: AI agents were getting incredibly capable — they could write code, plan tasks, and reason through problems — but they were blind to the one screen that matters most: your phone. Debugging a mobile app meant endlessly describing what you saw, taking screenshots by hand, dragging them into a chat, and repeating that loop dozens of times a day.

So we built the bridge. First a floating bubble that captured any screen in one tap. Then a tiny hub that received frames over the local network. Then the Model Context Protocol arrived and everything clicked: with MCP, any agent could not only see the phone but also act on it — tap, swipe, type, inspect the UI tree, compare frames, read logs.

Today ScreenSync MCP is a full pipeline: a polished Flutter app, a Node.js hub with 226 MCP tools (29 for your phone and desktop, 197 for your browser), a Chrome and Edge extension, live SSE streaming with real latency stats, QR pairing, and a privacy architecture where your data never touches our servers — because we don't have any. The one hop you choose is your AI agent: screenshots it asks for go to its model provider. Read where we're heading on our Goal & Vision page, or set up ScreenSync MCP with Claude.

What we believe

  • Local-first is the future

    The most trustworthy cloud is the one you don't need. Your screen data belongs on your hardware.

  • Open standards win

    We bet on MCP instead of a proprietary API, so ScreenSync works with every agent — today's and tomorrow's.

  • Craft matters

    Every screen is tested down to 360dp, every animation earns its place, every latency millisecond is measured.
